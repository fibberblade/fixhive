Models We Cover

Lamborghini Models We Service in Dubai

Urus / Urus Performante / Urus SE

The Lamborghini Super SUV — hugely popular in the UAE. Shares platform with Audi RS Q8 and Porsche Cayenne Turbo. Known faults: air suspension compressor wear in UAE heat, 4.0L twin-turbo V8 oil change intervals critically short in extreme heat (every 7,500 km max), and carbon ceramic brake (CCBC) dust contaminating callipers.

Huracán EVO / STO / Tecnica

The V10 supercar. Known faults: clutch wear accelerated by Dubai stop-start traffic (dual-clutch LDF transmission), 12V battery drain when parked in UAE heat for extended periods, and oil temperature running high in congested conditions requiring cooling system checks.

Revuelto

The successor to the Aventador — 6.5L V12 + hybrid system. Known faults: first services are critical for V12 seating, PHEV battery thermal management in UAE heat, and carbon fibre body panel care in dusty conditions.

Why FixHive

Why Choose FixHive for Your Lamborghini in Dubai?

Lamborghini in Dubai is not rare — the Urus in particular is a common sight, and Huracán and Aventador/Revuelto models are well represented. What is rare is an independent garage in the UAE with the equipment and expertise to service them correctly. FixHive uses ODIS (Offboard Diagnostic and Information System) — the same Volkswagen Group diagnostic platform used for Lamborghini, Audi, Porsche, and Bentley — giving full access to every ECU, including the Urus’s Lamborghini-specific Dinamica Veicolo Integrata (LDI) dynamics system.

The Urus shares its platform with the Audi RS Q8, Porsche Cayenne Turbo, and Bentley Bentayga — all sharing the MLB Evo architecture. This means components, service procedures, and diagnostic protocols are consistent. FixHive services all four makes, giving us deep familiarity with the platform in UAE conditions specifically.

Lamborghini authorised service in Dubai commands some of the highest dealer fees in the region. FixHive provides ODIS diagnostic capability and OEM-equivalent parts at a fraction of authorised dealer pricing — without compromising on the quality of fluids, parts, or procedures. We use Castrol EDGE 0W-40 or equivalent for Urus V8 services and Lamborghini-specified LDF fluid for all dual-clutch gearbox services.
