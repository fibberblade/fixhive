Common Issues

Common Toyota Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Land Cruiser & Prado Suspension Wear

The GX470, 200 Series and J150 Prado are popular choices in Dubai but the suspension components — particularly upper control arm bushings, shock absorbers and ball joints — take heavy wear from UAE road conditions. We stock common suspension parts for these models and can often complete the repair same-day.

⚠️ Camry & Corolla Transmission Issues

Automatic gearbox slipping, harsh shifts and shudder on takeoff are common in higher-mileage Camry and Corolla models in the UAE. We diagnose whether the issue is a software update, valve body repair, or a fluid change before recommending a full rebuild — saving you unnecessary cost.

⚠️ Toyota AC Compressor Failure

Toyota compressors — particularly on the Fortuner and older Land Cruiser models — are prone to failure when the vehicle is used in stop-start Dubai traffic with the AC running continuously at maximum load. We regas, rebuild and replace Toyota compressors using quality replacement units.

⚠️ Corolla & Yaris Engine Oil Consumption

Some Toyota petrol engines, particularly the 2ZR-FE engine in the Corolla, are known to consume oil between services. Catching this early with regular oil level checks prevents piston ring and valve stem seal damage. We monitor and advise appropriately.
