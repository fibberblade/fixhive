Our Story

Built on Trust. Driven by Expertise.

FixHive Garage (Saher Auto General Repairing LLC) has been serving Dubai's drivers for over 18 years. What started as a small workshop has grown into one of Dubai's most trusted independent garages — built entirely on word of mouth and a commitment to honest, dealer-quality service.

We noticed that UAE car owners faced a tough choice: pay dealer prices, or risk an unreliable independent shop. We built FixHive to be the third option — certified expertise, genuine parts, and transparent pricing you can trust.

Today we service everything from everyday Japanese and Korean cars to European performance vehicles and modern EVs — all under one roof, with the same care every time.
