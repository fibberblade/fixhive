Chery Repair & Service in Dubai

Dubai specialists for Chery Tiggo 8, Tiggo 7, Arrizo 6 and Omoda 5 — turbocharged engine servicing, CVT and DCT gearbox care, ADAS and AC maintenance.

Chery Specialists in Dubai

Tiggo 8 Pro · Tiggo 7 Pro · Tiggo 4 Pro · Arrizo 6 Pro · Omoda 5

Chery has established a meaningful foothold in the UAE automotive market, particularly with the Tiggo 8 Pro — a seven-seat SUV that offers premium European-influenced styling and a long feature list at a price point significantly below European and Japanese competitors. The Omoda 5 has more recently generated strong interest from younger buyers seeking a stylish crossover with modern technology at an accessible price. As Chery's UAE fleet grows, the need for skilled independent service — beyond the limited dealer network — has increased.

Chery's current UAE lineup uses a 1.6T turbocharged petrol engine (the SQRE4T16D) in most Tiggo and Arrizo models, producing 145–197 horsepower depending on tune. This engine pairs with either a CVT (continuous variable transmission) or a 7DCT dual-clutch gearbox. The CVT variant offers smooth urban driving but requires Chery-specified CVT fluid replacement every 60,000 km — a service that is often overlooked. The 7DCT is quicker-responding but requires wet clutch pack fluid at the same interval and a clutch adaptation reset after fluid changes.

Chery's ADAS systems on Pro models are comprehensive and well-integrated, but they require periodic alignment checks and camera calibration. The 360-degree camera system is particularly useful in Dubai's tight parking environments but requires calibration after any bumper repair or camera unit replacement. FixHive provides the complete range of Chery service, with technicians trained on all current Chery platforms and diagnostic tools that give full access to Chery's proprietary fault code architecture.

Common Chery Issues in Dubai

⚠️ CVT Transmission Overheating in Dubai Traffic

Chery's CVT-equipped Tiggo 8 and Arrizo 6 models use a belt-driven continuously variable transmission that is particularly sensitive to fluid condition in Dubai's climate. The prolonged stop-start driving that characterises Dubai city traffic keeps the CVT under near-continuous load at low belt-to-pulley speed ratios — the most thermally demanding operating condition for this type of gearbox. CVT fluid at incorrect level or beyond service life causes belt slippage, pulley wear and in extreme cases complete CVT failure. We recommend Chery CVT fluid changes every 40,000 km in UAE conditions rather than the factory 60,000 km specification.

⚠️ 1.6T Engine Carbon Buildup on Intake Valves

Direct-injection turbocharged engines like Chery's 1.6T TGDI are prone to carbon buildup on the backs of the intake valves because fuel is injected directly into the combustion chamber rather than washing the valves as in port-injected engines. In Dubai's dusty environment this process is accelerated by air filter degradation, which allows fine particles to enter the intake path and adhere to the carbon deposits. Symptoms include rough idle, hesitation on acceleration and a minor power reduction. We offer a walnut blasting intake valve cleaning service that removes these deposits without engine disassembly.

⚠️ Air Conditioning Evaporator & Compressor Issues

Dubai's climate demands maximum AC output for 8 months of the year, which places the Chery AC system under sustained stress. The evaporator — mounted inside the dashboard in a relatively tight space — is susceptible to mould growth in the humid UAE coastal environment when the car is parked for extended periods without the AC running the dry-out cycle. We perform an evaporator sterilisation service using ozone treatment and carry out a full refrigerant pressure test at every major service. The AC compressor belt tension is also checked at every service — a loose belt causes compressor clutch slippage and premature wear.

⚠️ Suspension & Steering Wear on UAE Roads

UAE road surfaces, while generally well-maintained, include a significant number of speed bumps and occasional sections of rough highway surface. Chery's Tiggo 8 and Tiggo 7, despite their relatively large wheel/tyre fitments, have front lower control arm bushings and front sway bar links that wear faster in UAE conditions than in temperate markets. Symptoms include a clunking noise over speed bumps, increased steering wander and tyre wear that is uneven across the tread width. We carry Chery-compatible suspension components in stock and align all four wheels after any suspension component replacement.
