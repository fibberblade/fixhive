Honda Service Centre Dubai

Honda has built its reputation on engineering reliability and driver-focused dynamics, and the Accord, Civic, CR-V and HR-V are popular choices across Dubai. Honda vehicles are generally dependable but require correct maintenance procedures and Honda-specific diagnostic equipment to service accurately — generic scanners often miss Honda-specific fault codes and adaptation requirements.



FixHive Garage uses HDS (Honda Diagnostic System) compatible equipment to accurately diagnose and repair all Honda models. Our technicians are familiar with Honda's engineering conventions and stock common parts for the UAE-specification models most frequently seen in Dubai.

Common Issues

Common Honda Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Honda CVT Transmission Issues

The CVT fitted to the HR-V, Jazz and some CR-V variants can develop shuddering and slipping in Dubai's stop-start traffic. Regular CVT fluid changes (every 40,000 km) are critical. We diagnose CVT faults and carry out the correct fluid change procedure with Honda-specified HCF-2 fluid.

⚠️ Honda Earth Dream Turbo Engine (1.5T)

The 1.5T VTEC Turbo engine in the Civic and CR-V can experience oil dilution in certain operating conditions — particularly short trips with extended low-load running. We advise on correct operating practices and oil change intervals to minimise this issue.

⚠️ Honda AC Evaporator Leaks

Older Honda Accord and Civic models are prone to evaporator core leaks causing gradual refrigerant loss and reduced cooling. We locate the leak, repair or replace the evaporator, and recharge the system — properly diagnosing the root cause rather than just topping up refrigerant.

⚠️ Suspension Wear (Lower Arm Bushings)

Front lower arm bushings and compliance bushings are a common wear item on Accord and Civic models in Dubai. Clunking over speed bumps and vague steering are the symptoms. We replace Honda suspension bushings with quality OEM-grade components.
