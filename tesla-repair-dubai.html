Common Issues

Common Tesla Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Door Handle Failures (Model S & Model X)

The motorised auto-presenting door handles on Model S and Model X are a well-known failure point. The electric motor and gear mechanism wear over time, leaving handles stuck retracted or partially extended. We source quality replacement handle assemblies and complete the repair without Tesla network access.

⚠️ 12V Battery Failure

All Tesla models use a separate 12V battery to power low-voltage systems. This battery fails like any conventional car battery — typically every 3–5 years. A dead 12V battery leaves the car completely unresponsive. We replace Tesla 12V batteries with the correct specification for your model year.

⚠️ Air Suspension Faults (Model S & Model X)

The air suspension on Model S and Model X develops compressor faults, air bag leaks and height sensor failures — particularly in Dubai's heat. Symptoms include uneven ride height, suspension warning messages, or the car lowering overnight. We diagnose and repair the specific failed component.

⚠️ Accelerated Tyre Wear

Teslas are significantly heavier than equivalent ICE vehicles and deliver torque instantly — causing faster tyre wear, especially on the rear axle. Dubai's hot roads compound this. Model 3 and Model Y owners frequently need tyres every 20,000–30,000 km. We stock Tesla-compatible tyre sizes and offer rotation with every visit.
