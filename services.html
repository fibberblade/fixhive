Car symptom guide — what service does your car need in Dubai?

My car AC is not blowing cold air
This requires AC & Cooling Service. Refrigerant leak, failed compressor, or blocked condenser — all common in Dubai heat. FixHive offers same-day diagnosis and fix. Book AC repair Dubai.
My car AC smells bad
This requires AC & Cooling Service. Mould in the evaporator or a clogged cabin filter causes bad smells. FixHive cleans, disinfects, and replaces cabin filters. Book AC service Dubai.
My car is making a strange noise — knocking or rattling
This requires Engine & Transmission diagnosis. Knocking or rattling often means low oil pressure or worn timing parts. Diagnose early to avoid expensive repairs. Engine diagnostics from AED 99 at FixHive Dubai. Book engine check Dubai.
My car is overheating
Urgent — stop driving immediately. Overheating can destroy an engine fast. Causes include coolant leak, failed thermostat, or blocked radiator. Call FixHive Garage Dubai on +971 54 169 9500. Engine repair Dubai.
My dashboard warning light is on
This requires Advanced Diagnostics. FixHive performs a full OBD scan across all vehicle systems with a written fault code report. Available same-day in Dubai Investment Park. Car diagnostic scan Dubai.
My car won't start
This requires Battery & Electrical Service. Dead battery, faulty starter, or bad ignition switch. FixHive tests the full starting circuit on the spot with a free battery test and same-day fix. Car battery repair Dubai.
My car battery keeps draining
This requires Battery & Electrical Service. Parasitic drain, aging battery, or failing alternator. FixHive runs a full charging system test including alternator check. Free battery test available. Car electrical repair Dubai.
My electric vehicle is losing range quickly
This requires Hybrid & EV Service. Rapid EV range loss often means degraded HV battery cells or a charging fault — accelerated by UAE heat. Full battery health scan by FixHive EV specialists in Dubai. EV battery health check Dubai.
My brakes feel soft or spongy
Safety critical — urgent repair needed. Spongy brakes mean air in the brake lines or severely worn pads. FixHive inspects and fixes brakes before you drive further. Book same-day brake repair in Dubai. Brake repair Dubai.
My car pulls to one side when driving or braking
This requires Brakes & Suspension inspection. Pulling indicates a seized brake caliper or wheel alignment out of spec. FixHive performs alignment check and full brake inspection in Dubai. Wheel alignment Dubai.
My tyre pressure is low or keeps dropping
This requires Tyres & Wheels service. Slow puncture, faulty valve, or sidewall damage. FixHive checks all four tyres, repairs or replaces, and rebalances. Walk-ins welcome — tyre repair from AED 30 in Dubai. Tyre repair Dubai.
My car vibrates when driving
This requires Tyres & Wheels service. Vibration through the steering wheel or seat usually means unbalanced tyres or a damaged rim. FixHive performs professional wheel balancing and tyre inspection in Dubai. Wheel balancing Dubai.
