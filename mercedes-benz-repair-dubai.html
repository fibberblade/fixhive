Common Issues

Common Mercedes-Benz Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Air Suspension Failure (Airmatic)

The Airmatic air suspension system fitted to E-Class, S-Class, GLE and GLS models is a common fault point in Dubai. Heat accelerates compressor wear and air bag degradation. Symptoms include a sagging or uneven ride height, compressor running continuously, or suspension warning lights. We repair and replace Airmatic components correctly.

⚠️ Mercedes 7G-Tronic Gearbox Issues

The 7G-Tronic automatic gearbox requires regular fluid changes (contrary to the 'lifetime fluid' myth). Harsh shifts, slipping, and shuddering are early symptoms of a fluid and filter service overdue. We also handle conductor plate replacements and valve body repairs on these gearboxes.

⚠️ Mercedes M264 Turbo Petrol Timing Chain Issues

The 2.0-litre M264 turbocharged petrol engine fitted to C200, E200 and GLC 200 models can develop timing chain guide wear over high mileages — especially in UAE heat where oil viscosity is stressed. Rattling on startup and rough running are warning signs. Early diagnosis and repair is critical — this damage escalates quickly if ignored.

⚠️ COMAND & Electronics Faults

Mercedes COMAND, MBUX and various body control modules can develop faults requiring specialist programming and reset procedures. We handle control module replacement, coding and software updates for Mercedes electronics across all model years.
