Car Detailing & Paint Protection in Dubai

From a deep interior clean to a full ceramic coat — we restore your car's finish and protect it against Dubai's heat, sand and UV for years to come.

⚠️ Signs You May Need This Service

Paint looks dull, faded or has lost its gloss
Fine swirl marks visible in direct sunlight
Water spots or mineral deposits on paint and glass
Interior smells stale, dusty or has food/coffee stains
Leather seats are dry, cracked or stained
Headlights are yellowed and hazy — reducing night visibility
Tar spots, tree sap or bird droppings etched into paint
You want to protect a new car's paint from Dubai's climate

Why Detailing Matters More in Dubai

A car in Dubai ages faster externally than almost anywhere else. UV intensity at this latitude degrades clear coat at roughly twice the rate of northern Europe. Sand particles suspended in the air and deposited on surfaces act as a slow-motion sandblaster on paint every time the wind blows. Automated car washes — the most common washing method in Dubai — use stiff brushes that leave swirl marks with every wash. Without protection, a new car's paint can look noticeably dull within 18–24 months.

Ceramic coating is the most effective protection currently available for Dubai conditions. A properly applied ceramic coat creates a hard, chemically bonded layer over the clear coat that reflects UV, repels water and contaminants, and has a hardness that resists light scratches. Quality ceramic coatings applied by trained applicators last 2–5 years in Dubai conditions. Cheap coatings sold over the counter and applied without proper surface preparation last months — not years.

PPF (Paint Protection Film) takes this a step further by providing physical protection against stone chips, car park scratches and minor abrasions. Self-healing PPF (which uses heat to recover light scratches) is particularly well suited to Dubai where the constant sun provides free healing energy. We apply PPF to high-impact areas — the full bonnet, front bumper, door edges and wing mirrors — where stone damage is most common.

Interior detailing in Dubai is important year-round because cars act as ovens when parked. Dashboard plastics become sticky, leather dries and cracks, and organic material in carpets and seats accelerates mould growth. A professional deep clean with steam extraction removes embedded contaminants that surface cleaning misses entirely, followed by UV-resistant conditioner on leather and plastics.

Our Process

How We Diagnose & Fix the Problem

1
Decontamination Wash

We use a pH-neutral two-bucket wash, followed by iron fallout remover and clay bar treatment to remove all embedded surface contaminants before any polishing begins. Polishing over contamination just scratches the paint.

2
Paint Inspection & Measurement

We measure paint thickness across all panels and inspect under LED lighting to assess scratch depth, oxidation level and determine what polishing intensity is appropriate without removing more clear coat than necessary.

3
Machine Polishing

We use dual-action and rotary polishers with appropriate compound and pad combinations for the condition found. The goal is to remove scratches and oxidation while preserving maximum clear coat thickness for future protection.

4
Protection Application

Depending on the package, we apply ceramic coating, paint sealant or carnauba wax. Ceramic coating requires 12–24 hours of cure time before the car should get wet — we advise you accordingly.

5
Interior Detail

Vacuum, steam clean seats and carpets, wipe all hard surfaces with appropriate interior detailer, condition leather and plastics, clean glass inside and out. Odour treatment if required.

Car Detailing Pricing in Dubai

A basic exterior wash and interior vacuum — what most people mean by a "car wash" — costs AED 30–80 at a standard wash station. That's maintenance cleaning, not detailing. A proper exterior detail — clay bar decontamination, machine polish to remove swirl marks and light scratches, and wax or sealant protection — typically costs AED 400–900 for a standard car. Larger SUVs and pickup trucks are at the higher end due to panel area.

Paint correction — a multi-stage machine polish to remove deep swirls, water spots, and oxidation — is a more intensive process costing AED 800–2,000 depending on paint condition and the number of polish stages required. Heavily swirled or sun-oxidised paint in Dubai (extremely common given the UV intensity) often needs a two-stage correction to properly restore clarity. Paint correction before ceramic coating is essential — coating over swirled paint permanently locks in those defects.

Ceramic coating costs depend heavily on product tier and coverage. Entry-level ceramic coatings for the full exterior run AED 800–1,400; professional-grade coatings with longer durability and higher hardness ratings run AED 1,500–3,500. Paint protection film (PPF) on the front end typically adds AED 1,800–3,500 on top of that for the bonnet, fenders and front bumper. In Dubai's environment, the combination of paint correction, ceramic coating, and front-end PPF is the most comprehensive long-term paint protection available.

Interior detailing — deep cleaning of fabric, leather, plastics and carpets — starts at AED 250–500 for a standard interior. Leather conditioning and odour treatment are additional services. All pricing is confirmed before work begins.

Window tinting is a common request in Dubai and is legally regulated by the RTA. Front side windows must allow at least 50% light transmission (VLT); rear windows and the rear windscreen have no VLT restriction. Quality ceramic window tint — which rejects significantly more heat than standard dyed film — typically costs AED 600–1,400 for a full car tint depending on film brand and car size. Cheap metalized films can interfere with GPS and radio signals; ceramic films do not. For Dubai's summer heat, the heat rejection difference between cheap and quality tint is very noticeable inside the car.

Engine bay cleaning and degreasing is often forgotten but matters for detecting oil leaks and for resale value. A thorough engine bay detail costs AED 150–250. We use low-pressure steam cleaning rather than high-pressure water to avoid displacing water into sensitive electronics. A clean engine bay makes it immediately obvious whether the engine is dry or has any developing leaks — which is valuable both for maintenance and when selling the car.

What Dubai Drivers Get Wrong About Detailing & Paint Protection

Myth: "Ceramic coating makes the car scratch-proof."

Ceramic coating adds significant hardness to the paint surface — it resists light swirl marks from improper washing far better than unprotected paint. But it does not prevent scratches from keys, sand particles blown at speed, or car park incidents. It also doesn't prevent water spots if water is allowed to dry on the surface in Dubai's mineral-rich water conditions. What ceramic coating genuinely excels at is chemical resistance, UV protection, and making the car much easier to wash clean. It's protection, not armour.

Myth: "Automatic car washes are fine for regular cleaning."

Automatic brush washes are one of the leading causes of swirl marks and micro-scratches on cars in Dubai. The brushes trap grit from previous cars and drag it across your paint at speed. Even touchless washes use high-pressure water that can drive sand particles into the paint surface. Proper hand washing using the two-bucket method with a clean microfibre wash mitt causes far less paint damage. If you use a car wash service, confirm it's a hand-wash operation using clean mitts changed between cars.

Myth: "PPF yellows and looks worse than bare paint within a year or two."

Early-generation PPF films did yellow with UV exposure, which gave the product a poor reputation. Modern premium PPF films (from established brands) include UV inhibitors that prevent yellowing for seven to ten years under normal conditions. Incorrect installation — trapped moisture, improper edge sealing — can cause lifting and discolouration, which is why installation quality matters as much as film quality. A properly installed modern PPF film is effectively invisible and preserves paint in Dubai's harsh UV environment far better than any coating alone.
