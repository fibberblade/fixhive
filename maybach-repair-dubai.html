Maybach Models We Service in Dubai

Mercedes-Maybach S 580 / S 680

Ultra-luxury saloon flagship. The S 680 uses a 6.0L V12 bi-turbo. Known faults: V12 oil consumption between services requires level checks every 2,000 km, rear axle steering module calibration drift, and Burmester 3D sound amplifier overheating in UAE heat.

Mercedes-Maybach GLS 600

Luxury full-size SUV — one of the most common Maybachs in the UAE. Known faults: E-Active Body Control hydraulic suspension requires specialist fluid and calibration service, 4.0L V8 bi-turbo intercooler cleaning, and rear massage seat rail corrosion in UAE humidity.

Mercedes-Maybach EQS 680 SUV

Electric Maybach SUV. Known faults: high-voltage battery range reduction in UAE summer (up to 20%), air suspension calibration with heavy loads, and Hyperscreen display calibration in extreme temperature changes.

Why Choose FixHive for Your Mercedes-Maybach in Dubai?

Mercedes-Maybach represents the absolute pinnacle of the Mercedes-Benz range — and ownership in the UAE brings specific servicing requirements. The GLS 600 Maybach’s E-Active Body Control hydraulic suspension system is one of the most complex active suspension architectures in any production vehicle, and requires specialist diagnostic access and hydraulic fluid expertise. The S 680’s 6.0L V12 bi-turbo is one of the last production V12 saloon engines in the world and demands meticulous oil maintenance in UAE heat.

FixHive uses Mercedes-Benz XENTRY/DAS diagnostic platforms — the same tools used by authorised MB dealers — to access all Maybach-specific modules: E-Active Body Control, MBUX infotainment, Burmester sound system electronics, rear-axle steering, and the EQS 680 SUV's EV high-voltage system. We use MB-approved 0W-40 engine oil for V8 engines and 0W-30 for V12, in the correct MB specifications (MB 229.5 / 229.51 / 229.71).

Mercedes-Maybach authorised service in Dubai can exceed AED 10,000 for a major service. FixHive provides the same XENTRY diagnostic depth and Mercedes-specification fluids at an independent rate — with the discretion and attention to detail that Maybach ownership deserves.
