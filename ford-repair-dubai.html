Common Issues

Common Ford Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Ford EcoBoost Turbo & Carbon Buildup

The 2.3L and 2.7L EcoBoost turbocharged engines are direct injection — meaning carbon buildup on intake valves is an inherent characteristic. On higher mileage EcoBoost engines this causes hesitation, misfires and rough idle. We perform intake cleaning and can address the root cause with catch can solutions.

⚠️ Ford 10-Speed Automatic Gearbox Shudder

The 10R80 10-speed automatic gearbox fitted to F-150, Mustang and Explorer models can develop shuddering during light acceleration. Ford has released multiple software calibration updates and specific torque converter fluid to address this. We perform the correct update procedure using IDS.

⚠️ Ford F-150 & Expedition Cooling Issues

The 5.0L Coyote V8 and 3.5L EcoBoost V6 in the F-150 and Expedition can develop cooling system issues at higher mileages — particularly coolant leaks from the degas bottle and thermostat housing. Dubai's heat accelerates these failures.

⚠️ Mustang Suspension & Alignment

The Mustang GT's live rear axle (Pre-2015) or independent rear suspension (S550/S650) requires correct alignment settings to prevent tyre wear and maintain handling. Dubai's speed humps and road conditions can knock alignment and damage lower control arm components.
