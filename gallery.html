A Real Look Inside FixHive Garage, Dubai Investment Park

Every photo in this gallery is taken inside our workshop at Dubai Investment Park First (DIP 1). We don't use stock images — what you see is what you get. Our team of experienced mechanics works on everything from daily drivers to exotic supercars, using professional-grade equipment and genuine or OEM-equivalent parts.

FixHive Garage specialises in engine rebuilds, gearbox and transmission overhauls, brake and suspension repair, AC and cooling system service, full car diagnostics with OBD and advanced scan tools, tyre fitting and wheel alignment, battery and electrical repairs, car detailing and paint protection, and hybrid and EV servicing. We work on all major brands including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Porsche, Toyota, Nissan, Land Rover, Jeep, Ferrari and more.

Our workshop is equipped with four-post lifts, two-post lifts, a dedicated AC service bay, wheel alignment rig, and a detailing zone with extraction and coating equipment. Jobs are handled by brand-trained technicians and overseen by our senior workshop manager. Every car leaves with a full job report and a warranty on parts and labour.
