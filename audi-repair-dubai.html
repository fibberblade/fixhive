Common Issues

Common Audi Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Audi S Tronic / DSG Gearbox Issues

The DSG (Direct Shift Gearbox) in 7-speed dry and 6-speed wet variants is a common service point. Shuddering at low speeds, hesitation and rough shifts in stop-start Dubai traffic are early signs. Regular mechatronic service with correct DSG fluid and a proper adaptation reset resolves most issues.

⚠️ Audi Oil Consumption (TFSI Engines)

The 1.8T and 2.0T TFSI engines fitted to A4, A5, A6 and Q5 are known for elevated oil consumption between services. The cause is typically worn piston rings or valve stem seals. We test and advise on the correct remediation approach for your specific mileage and condition.

⚠️ Audi Air Suspension (CDC / Adaptive Air)

Air suspension fitted to A6, A8 and Q7 models can develop compressor, air bag and valve faults — particularly in the heat. Symptoms include uneven ride height, suspension warning lights, and compressor running constantly. We repair Audi air suspension correctly using quality replacement components.

⚠️ Carbon Buildup on GDI Engines

Direct injection TFSI engines don't spray fuel over the intake valves, meaning carbon deposits build up over time and restrict airflow. Symptoms include rough idle, misfires and reduced power. We perform intake valve walnut blasting — the correct long-term solution to GDI carbon buildup.
