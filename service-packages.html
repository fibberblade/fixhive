Frequently Asked Questions

FixCARE covers the essentials: engine oil and filter change using OEM-grade oil, top-up of all fluid levels, 20-point safety inspection, tyre pressure and tread check, brake visual inspection, lights and wipers check, and a service interval reset.

FixPRIME includes everything in FixCARE plus: air and cabin filter replacement, spark plug inspection or replacement, full OBD diagnostic scan, battery health test, brake fluid condition test, AC performance check, and a complimentary car wash.

FixELITE includes everything in FixPRIME plus: coolant system flush and refill, tyre rotation and balancing, advanced diagnostic deep scan, priority booking and scheduling, complimentary collection and delivery, interior vacuum and wipe-down, and a 12-month service record update.
