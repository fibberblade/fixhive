Common Issues

Common Nissan Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Nissan CVT Transmission Problems

The continuously variable transmission (CVT) fitted to Nissan X-Trail, Qashqai and Altima models is a known issue point — particularly when overheated in Dubai traffic. Symptoms include shuddering, slipping and hesitation. Regular CVT fluid changes (every 40,000 km) are essential to avoid expensive replacement.

⚠️ Patrol Y62 Engine & Cooling Issues

The VK56VD V8 in the Patrol Y62 is a strong engine but requires regular service and coolant system attention. Thermostat failures, water pump leaks and radiator blockages are common in high-mileage examples. We service Patrol V8 engines correctly with the right spec oils and fluids.

⚠️ Nissan AC Compressor Failure

Nissan AC compressors — particularly on the Sunny and older Altima — are prone to failure in Dubai's continuous AC use conditions. We regas and replace Nissan compressors using quality OEM-grade units, often completing the repair same-day.

⚠️ Timing Chain Issues (QR25 Engine)

The QR25DE engine found in the Altima and X-Trail has known timing chain rattle issues at cold start. Left unattended, the chain stretches and causes misfires and potential engine damage. We diagnose and replace Nissan timing chains using the correct OEM-specification components.
