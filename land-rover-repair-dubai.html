Common Issues

Common Land Rover Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Air Suspension Failure (EAS/AIRS)

Air suspension failure is the most common repair we carry out on Range Rover and Discovery models. Compressor failure, air bag splits, valve block leaks and height sensor faults all cause the vehicle to drop or sit unevenly. We diagnose and repair all EAS components, often saving significant cost versus dealer pricing.

⚠️ Land Rover Automatic Gearbox (ZF 8HP)

The ZF 8-speed automatic requires regular fluid changes — not 'lifetime' fluid as Land Rover claims. Harsh, jerky shifts, shuddering and gearbox warning lights are early signs. We service and repair ZF gearboxes with the correct Esso LT 71141 fluid and the proper fill procedure.

⚠️ Land Rover Cooling System Issues

The 3.0-litre supercharged petrol engine in Range Rover Sport and older full-size Range Rover models is prone to cooling system issues — particularly the thermostat housing and coolant pipe fittings. Overheating in Dubai traffic is a risk with a failing cooling system. We repair these before they escalate.

⚠️ Terrain Response & Transfer Box Faults

Terrain Response system faults, transfer case issues and front differential problems are common on Discovery and Defender models used for off-road driving. We diagnose and repair drivetrain faults using the correct specialist tools — not generic OBD scanners that miss these faults.
