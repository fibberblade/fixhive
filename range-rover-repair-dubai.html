Common Issues

Common Range Rover Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Air Suspension Failure (EAS / AIRS)

Air suspension failure is the single most common repair we carry out on Range Rover Sport and Vogue models. Compressor failure, air bag splits, valve block leaks, and height sensor faults all cause the vehicle to drop, sit unevenly, or display a suspension fault warning. We diagnose and repair all EAS components individually — saving significant cost compared to dealer replacement of entire assemblies.

⚠️ ZF 8-Speed Gearbox (8HP)

Range Rover Sport and full-size Range Rover models fitted with the ZF 8-speed automatic suffer harsh shifts, shuddering, and gearbox warnings when the transmission fluid degrades. Despite Land Rover's 'lifetime fluid' claim, we recommend regular ZF fluid changes using Esso LT 71141 fluid and the correct fill procedure to avoid expensive gearbox damage.

⚠️ Ingenium Engine Oil Consumption

The 3.0-litre Ingenium six-cylinder petrol found in Range Rover Sport, Velar, and the L460 full-size Range Rover can exhibit excessive oil consumption between service intervals. Left unchecked in Dubai's heat, this leads to low-oil warnings and potential engine damage. We diagnose the root cause and carry out the appropriate repair before it escalates.

⚠️ Cooling System & Supercharger (V8 Sport)

The 5.0-litre supercharged V8 in older Range Rover Sport models is prone to coolant pipe failures and supercharger intercooler issues, particularly in Dubai's summer heat. Overheating events can cause serious engine damage. We inspect, diagnose, and repair cooling systems proactively and in response to warning signs.
