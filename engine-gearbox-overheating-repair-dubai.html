Overheating & Cooling Repair

In Dubai's heat, overheating can destroy an engine in minutes. We diagnose the root cause — thermostat, radiator, water pump or head gasket — not just top up coolant.

Warning signs

  • Temperature gauge in the red
  • Steam or smoke from under the hood
  • Coolant leak or low coolant warning
  • Sweet burning smell from engine bay
  • Heater blowing cold air in cabin

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about engine, gearbox and overheating repair in Dubai.

How much does engine repair cost in Dubai? +
Engine diagnostics and a service run AED 250–600. Timing belt or chain work is AED 800–2,200. Head gasket repair is AED 2,500–6,000. A full engine overhaul or rebuild is typically AED 4,000–12,000 depending on engine size and make. FixHive provides a written quote before starting any work — no surprises.
How much does gearbox or transmission repair cost in Dubai? +
Transmission fluid changes run AED 400–900. A full gearbox overhaul is AED 3,500–9,000 depending on make and extent of wear. Manual clutch replacement is typically AED 900–2,500. All jobs include a written quote you approve before we start.
Why is my car overheating in Dubai? +
Dubai's extreme heat places severe pressure on cooling systems. Common causes include a failing thermostat, blocked or leaking radiator, failing water pump, degraded coolant or a blown head gasket. FixHive diagnoses the root cause — not just the symptom — so it doesn't happen again. Stop driving immediately if your temperature gauge is in the red.
Do you require advance payment for engine or gearbox repair? +
No. FixHive never requires advance payment. We diagnose, provide a written quote, you approve the work, and payment is only due on collection of your vehicle. This is a core commitment we make to every customer.
