Body Repair & Paint Services in Dubai

Dents, scratches, bumper damage — we restore your car's exterior to factory condition with colour-matched paintwork.

⚠️ Signs You May Need This Service

Scratches, scuffs or paint chips from kerbs or car parks
Dents or dings from minor collisions or door impacts
Paint fading, oxidising or losing gloss in the sun
Rust spots developing at the edges of chips or scratches
Panel gaps inconsistent after a previous repair
Body panel misalignment — doors, boot lid or bonnet don't sit flush
Water marks or mineral deposits on paint or glass
Cracked, chipped or damaged bumpers

Body & Paint Challenges in the UAE

Dubai's UV radiation is among the most intense in the world. Prolonged exposure causes paint oxidation — the clear coat breaks down, the colour coat underneath fades, and eventually you see a chalky, dull surface that no amount of waxing can restore. Modern paint protection (either ceramic coating or PPF film) is not a luxury in Dubai — it's the difference between a car that looks new after 5 years and one that looks 10 years old after 3.

Sand damage is subtle but cumulative. Every sandstorm, every drive through desert dust, every automated car wash with stiff brushes leaves microscopic scratches in the clear coat. Under direct sunlight you see them as a web of fine swirl marks. Machine polishing removes these scratches from the clear coat — but it only works while sufficient clear coat thickness remains. Once you've polished through to the base coat, the only fix is a full respray. This is why we recommend polishing before the damage gets too deep, not after.

Parking damage is a fact of life in Dubai's busy malls and residential underground garages. PDR (Paintless Dent Removal) is the right solution for most panel dents where the paint is intact — it's faster, cheaper and preserves the original factory paint. For dents with paint damage, traditional filler and respray is required, and we use a computerised colour-matching system to blend the new paint seamlessly with the original shade, even accounting for years of UV fading.

Our Process

How We Diagnose & Fix the Problem

1
Damage Assessment & Documentation

We photograph and document all damage before starting work. For insurance claims, we produce a formal written assessment with repair methodology and parts required.

2
Panel Straightening / PDR

For dents without paint damage we use PDR — pushing and massaging the metal back from behind the panel without disturbing the factory paint. For structural damage we use our frame-straightening bench to restore panel geometry.

3
Surface Preparation

Bare metal is cleaned, primed and blocked before any paint is applied. Skipping prep is why budget repairs fail — the paint looks fine for 6 months then starts to peel or show telegraph lines.

4
Computer Colour Match & Paint

We scan the existing paint with our spectrophotometer to get the exact colour code and formula, adjusted for fade. The new paint is mixed and applied in our filtered spray booth to prevent dust contamination.

5
Polish & Final Inspection

After paint has fully cured we compound and polish the repaired area to blend it with the surrounding panels. We check in natural light and under UV lamps to confirm the match before delivery.

Body Repair & Paint Pricing in Dubai

Minor dent and scratch repair pricing depends entirely on panel size, paint complexity and paint code availability. A small door ding with no paint transfer can be removed by PDR (paintless dent repair) for AED 150–350. A larger dent requiring filler and full panel respray typically costs AED 450–900 per panel. Bumper respray — one of the most common Dubai jobs given parking lot incidents — ranges from AED 350–700 depending on colour complexity and whether sensors need to be removed and refitted.

Full panel replacement is more expensive because parts cost significantly more for premium brands. A front wing for a Toyota Corolla or Nissan Altima might run AED 300–600 for the part; the equivalent panel for a BMW 5 Series or Mercedes E-Class can be AED 800–2,000 before labour and paint. We always advise whether repair or replacement is better value — for many panel dents, repair is technically superior to replacement when done well.

Paint protection film (PPF) on the full front end — bonnet, fenders, front bumper — typically costs AED 1,800–3,500 for quality film professionally installed. Ceramic coating starts at around AED 800–2,500 depending on coverage and product tier. Dubai's sandstorms and construction dust make paint protection a genuinely worthwhile investment for cars driven in the emirate long-term.

All body repair quotes include an exact breakdown of materials, labour and paint. Colour matching is done with a spectrophotometer so the repaired panel blends properly — no guesswork, no visible repair line.

What Dubai Drivers Get Wrong About Body Repair

Myth: "Touch-up paint from a pen is good enough for stone chips."

Touch-up pens fill the chip but never match the factory paint precisely — the texture and sheen are different, leaving a visible dot or smear. More importantly, touch-up paint applied over a chip where bare metal is exposed does not properly seal out moisture. In Dubai's coastal humidity, trapped moisture under touch-up paint can start rust within months — rare but not impossible, especially on bare steel panels. Proper chip repair involves etching, priming and colour-matched paint application in layers.

Myth: "Respraying a panel devalues the car more than leaving the damage."

A poor respray — wrong colour match, orange-peel texture, visible blend line — does devalue a car. A professional respray with proper colour matching and blending into adjacent panels is indistinguishable from factory paint and restores value. The real risk is choosing the cheapest option. Inspect previous work, look for blend-panel transitions, and check the paint under a light at a flat angle. At FixHive we stand behind every paint job with a workmanship guarantee.

Myth: "PPF is only for supercars and luxury vehicles."

Paint protection film makes financial sense on any car you intend to keep for more than three years. Dubai's sand-laden winds, gravel from construction traffic, and the intensity of UV radiation cause paint fading and micro-scratching that compound over time. A family SUV driven in Dubai for five years without protection will show noticeably more paint degradation than one with even a partial PPF application on the leading surfaces. The cost of a front-end PPF application is typically less than a full respray of the same panels.
