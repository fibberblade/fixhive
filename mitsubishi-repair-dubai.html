Common Issues

Common Mitsubishi Problems in Dubai

⚠️ CVT Transmission Shudder (Outlander & Eclipse Cross)

CVT shudder and hesitation under light acceleration are the most common complaints on current Mitsubishi Outlander and Eclipse Cross models. The root cause is almost always degraded CVT fluid — Mitsubishi specifies a unique fluid (CVTF-J4 or equivalent) that must not be substituted with generic CVT fluid. Incorrect fluid causes irreversible wear. We carry out the full CVT service with the correct fluid and adaptation reset.

⚠️ Pajero Super Select 4WD System Wear

The Pajero's Super Select II 4WD system is one of the most capable on the market but requires regular transfer case and front/rear differential fluid changes — particularly if the vehicle has seen off-road use. Worn or contaminated fluid causes notchy engagement, vibration in 4WD mode and premature wear of the centre differential. We service the complete 4WD drivetrain on schedule.

⚠️ Cooling System Failures (High-Mileage Pajero)

Higher-mileage Pajeros — particularly the 3.0L and 3.8L V6 — develop thermostat failures, water pump leaks and radiator hose degradation. Dubai's heat accelerates the ageing of rubber seals and plastic coolant fittings. A cooling system inspection at 100,000 km is strongly recommended. We pressure-test and service Mitsubishi cooling systems completely.

⚠️ AC System Failures in Peak Summer

Mitsubishi AC systems are well-designed but the UAE's near-continuous AC operation puts compressors, condensers and refrigerant circuits under significant stress. Compressor clutch failures and refrigerant leaks from O-ring degradation are the most common causes of AC failure on Outlander and Pajero. Annual pre-summer inspection prevents most failures.
