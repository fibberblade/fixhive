Common Issues

Common Lexus Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Hybrid Battery Degradation (RX 450h, ES 300h, NX 300h)

Dubai's extreme heat accelerates hybrid battery degradation more than any other factor. Symptoms include reduced fuel economy, reduced EV-mode driving, and the hybrid battery warning light. We perform Lexus hybrid battery health checks, individual cell testing, and targeted cell replacement — significantly cheaper than full pack replacement.

⚠️ AC Evaporator Leaks (Older RX & ES)

Lexus AC evaporator failures are a recurring repair on higher-mileage RX 300/350 and ES 330/350 models. The evaporator core develops micro-leaks over time, causing refrigerant loss and a distinctive sweet smell inside the cabin. Replacement requires dashboard removal — a labour-intensive job we carry out correctly and completely.

⚠️ Air Suspension Faults (LX 570 / LX 600)

The LX uses an air suspension system that develops height sensor faults, compressor wear and air spring leaks — particularly in Dubai's heat cycle conditions. Symptoms include uneven ride height, a suspension warning message and the car sitting lower on one corner. We diagnose the specific failed component rather than replacing the entire system.

⚠️ Automatic Transmission Hesitation (V6 Petrol Models)

Lexus V6 automatics on older RX and ES models can develop hesitation, flare on upshifts, and shudder on light acceleration — often caused by degraded transmission fluid or a worn torque converter. A fluid service at the correct interval prevents the majority of these issues. We service Lexus transmissions using the correct Toyota WS specification fluid.
