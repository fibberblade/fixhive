Lexus Service Centre Dubai

Common Issues

Common Lexus Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Hybrid Battery Degradation in Dubai Heat

The nickel-metal hydride (NiMH) and lithium-ion hybrid batteries in the RX 450h, ES 300h, NX 450h+ and CT 200h are specifically vulnerable to accelerated capacity loss in Dubai's extreme summer temperatures. When the hybrid cooling system — which uses cabin air drawn through a duct behind the rear seat — becomes blocked with dust or the blower motor weakens, battery cell temperatures spike and trigger premature degradation. Symptoms include reduced EV range, increased fuel consumption, a triangle warning light, and a B0100 or P3000-range hybrid fault code. We inspect the hybrid battery cooling path, test individual cell balance with specialist equipment, and carry out inverter coolant refresh as required.

⚠️ GX 460 & LX Suspension Wear

The GX 460 and LX 570/600 are used extensively in the UAE's desert and off-road environment — conditions that accelerate wear on upper and lower control arm bushings, front strut bearings, sway bar links and steering rack components. In our experience, GX 460 models used on UAE graded tracks typically need front suspension component replacement at 60,000–80,000 km rather than the 120,000+ km expected in mild climates. We stock or can rapidly source OEM suspension parts for all Lexus GX and LX variants, and we perform four-wheel alignment on our touchless alignment system after every suspension repair.

⚠️ 2GR-FE & 2GR-FKS Timing Chain Stretch

The 3.5-litre 2GR-FE V6 engine used in older RX 350, IS 350, GS 350 and ES 350 models — and its newer 2GR-FKS D-4ST variant — can develop timing chain stretch and VVT actuator wear, particularly when oil change intervals have been extended or when the engine has seen sustained high-load driving in Dubai's heat. Early symptoms include a rattling noise from the front of the engine on cold start, a P0016 or P0017 VVT correlation code, and rough idle. Catching this early prevents catastrophic engine damage. We diagnose, replace chain components and re-time the engine as required.

⚠️ AC System Overload & Compressor Failure

Lexus vehicles driven year-round in Dubai run their air conditioning systems at maximum load for seven to eight months of the year. This places sustained stress on the AC compressor clutch, condenser fan motor and refrigerant circuit. The ES 300h and NX 300h use an electrically-driven compressor for hybrid AC operation, which requires specialist handling — a standard AC regas without checking compressor lubrication can damage the electric compressor irreparably. We service Lexus AC systems with the correct PAG oil grade and refrigerant, and test system pressure, condenser temperature and compressor operation before returning the vehicle.
