Common Issues

Common Hyundai Problems in Dubai

⚠️ Hyundai Theta II Engine Issues

The 2.4L Theta II GDI engine fitted to the Sonata and Santa Fe has known issues with bearing failure and oil consumption — leading to Hyundai issuing extended warranty coverage in some markets. We inspect and diagnose Theta II engines and advise on the correct remediation approach.

⚠️ Hyundai Dual-Clutch Transmission (DCT)

The 7-speed dry DCT fitted to Tucson, Elantra and Creta models can develop shuddering and hesitation, particularly at low speeds in stop-start Dubai traffic. Regular fluid changes and software updates from the correct diagnostic platform can resolve many issues before they escalate.

⚠️ AC System & Refrigerant Loss

Hyundai AC systems — particularly on older Sonata and Elantra models — can develop refrigerant leaks at the evaporator or condenser. We locate leaks using electronic detection, repair the source, and recharge the system to manufacturer specifications.

⚠️ Suspension Noise & Wear

Front strut mounts, anti-roll bar links and lower arm bushings are common wear items on Tucson and Santa Fe models in Dubai's road conditions. We diagnose suspension noise accurately and repair using quality replacement components.
