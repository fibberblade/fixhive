Models We Cover

Ferrari Models We Service in Dubai

Roma / Portofino M

Ferrari's entry-level GT and convertible. Known faults: 3.9L twin-turbo V8 oil change intervals critically short in UAE heat (every 8,000 km), DCT dual-clutch pack wear in Dubai stop-start traffic, and 12V battery drain when parked.

SF90 Stradale / XX

Ferrari's PHEV flagship. Known faults: high-voltage battery thermal management in UAE summer heat, carbon ceramic brake contamination from sand, and tyre wear on 21-inch Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 in UAE temperatures.

296 GTB / GTS

The 3.0L twin-turbo V6 + hybrid. Known faults: PHEV battery range reduction in heat, intercooler efficiency reduction, and DCT paddle shifter calibration in extreme temperatures.

Purosangue

Ferrari's first SUV — 6.5L naturally aspirated V12. Known faults: oil level monitoring critical (V12 consumes more in heat), air suspension setup critical for correct ride, and first services must be done on time.

F8 Tributo / Spider

Mid-engine sports car. Known faults: 3.9L twin-turbo oil seal leaks on high-mileage examples, spider roof hydraulics (convertible), and cam cover gaskets on extended service intervals.

Why FixHive

Why Choose FixHive for Your Ferrari in Dubai?

Ferrari ownership in Dubai carries unique service challenges. The extreme ambient heat (45°C+) creates conditions the engines were not primarily designed for — oil service intervals must be shortened, tyre compounds behave differently, and brake systems run hotter than in the European conditions where most Ferrari development occurs. FixHive technicians understand these UAE-specific factors and adapt service procedures accordingly.

We use Ferrari SD (System Diagnosis) compatible diagnostic tools and Autel MaxiSys Ultra to access all Ferrari ECUs — F1 dual-clutch gearbox, chassis and suspension, KERS/hybrid systems on SF90 and 296 GTB, climate control, and all body electronics. Oil services use Ferrari-specified Esso Ultron 0W-40 or equivalent Shell/Castrol Ferrari-approved grades in the correct specification for each engine variant.

Ferrari authorised service in Dubai is among the most expensive in the region — and service slot availability can be limited. FixHive offers the same technical capability at an independent rate with direct access to the technician, faster turnaround, and honest communication about what is genuinely required versus what is upsold. Many UAE Ferrari owners use FixHive for routine services while returning to the dealer only for warranty-specific interventions.
