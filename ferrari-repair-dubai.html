Ferrari Repair & Service in Dubai

Specialist Ferrari care in Dubai for Roma, Portofino M, SF90 Stradale, F8 Tributo and 296 GTB. Prancing Horse expertise, rare parts sourcing and value-preserving service.

Ferrari Specialists in Dubai

Roma · Portofino M · SF90 Stradale · F8 Tributo · 296 GTB

Ferrari is the ultimate expression of Italian automotive art, and Dubai is one of Ferrari's most important global markets. The combination of wealth, a culture that celebrates exceptional vehicles and roads that genuinely permit spirited driving has made the UAE one of the highest concentrations of Prancing Horse ownership in the world. Every model in the current range demands service from technicians who respect and understand that heritage.

Ferrari's current lineup divides broadly into two powertrain families: the turbocharged V8 family used in the Roma, Portofino M, F8 Tributo and 296 GTB (in hybrid configuration), and the front-mid-mounted V12 in the 812 Superfast and GTC4Lusso. Both families deliver remarkable reliability when correctly maintained, but both require service from technicians who understand the specific requirements — oil specifications, torque values, calibration procedures — that Ferrari's engineering demands.

In Dubai's climate, the turbocharged V8 models face specific challenges from the heat. The turbochargers themselves require high-quality, correctly-specified engine oil in excellent condition. Oil soak periods after high-performance driving are important — the engine should be allowed to idle for a minute after hard driving before being switched off, allowing the turbocharger bearings to cool with flowing oil rather than coking. In UAE conditions where many Ferrari owners use their cars as daily drivers, this soak-down practice is easy to neglect.

Ferrari's residual value is exceptional, and maintaining a complete, correctly recorded service history is one of the most important factors in preserving it. A Ferrari with full documented service history from a reputable specialist commands a significant premium over one with incomplete records. FixHive provides fully itemised service records for every visit, using genuine or OEM-equivalent parts with full traceability.

Common Ferrari Issues in Dubai

⚠️ Turbocharger Oil Coking on V8 Models

Ferrari's twin-turbocharged V8 engines (F154 family) use turbochargers that operate at extreme temperatures. When the engine is switched off immediately after sustained performance driving without an oil soak period, the residual heat causes the oil trapped in the bearing journals to carbonise. Over time, coked oil restricts flow to the turbocharger bearings and causes premature wear. FixHive emphasises the correct shutdown procedure to every Ferrari owner and inspects turbocharger oil supply and return lines at every service.

⚠️ High-Voltage Battery Maintenance (SF90 and 296 GTB)

The SF90 Stradale and 296 GTB use plug-in hybrid systems with lithium-ion high-voltage batteries that require specialist care in Dubai's thermal environment. The battery cooling system must be maintained correctly, and the high-voltage battery should not be left at a full state of charge for extended periods in Dubai's heat. FixHive has high-voltage safety training and the diagnostic capability for Ferrari's hybrid systems.

⚠️ Carbon Ceramic Brake Inspection

Most current Ferrari models are specified with carbon ceramic brakes (CCM) that offer exceptional performance but require careful inspection in Dubai's conditions. Carbon ceramic rotors are vulnerable to thermal shock from rapid temperature changes and can develop surface micro-cracking if the car is driven hard then parked on cold marble flooring. FixHive inspects CCM rotor and pad condition at every service and advises on replacement intervals based on actual wear measurements.

⚠️ Cooling System Maintenance in UAE Heat

Ferrari's high-output engines generate significant heat amplified by Dubai's ambient temperatures. Coolant chemistry must be maintained precisely — Ferrari uses a specific coolant formulation that must not be mixed with standard products. The cooling fan system, thermostat and coolant pump are all items that FixHive inspects at every Ferrari service as standard, since a cooling failure on a Ferrari is a significantly more expensive repair than the prevention it avoids.
