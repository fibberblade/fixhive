Common Issues

Common Porsche Problems in Dubai

?? Porsche PDK Gearbox Issues

The 7-speed and 8-speed PDK fitted to 911, Cayenne, Macan and Panamera is a high-precision dual-clutch system. Hesitation, shuddering or harsh shifts — especially in slow Dubai traffic — are often early signs of fluid degradation or mechatronic wear. Regular PDK fluid changes and adaptation resets prevent the majority of failures.

?? Porsche PASM Air Suspension

Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) air suspension fitted to Cayenne and Panamera can develop compressor, air strut and valve block faults in Dubai's heat. Symptoms include uneven ride height, warning lights and a compressor that runs continuously. We diagnose and repair PASM systems with quality replacement components.

?? Porsche IMS Bearing (Older 911)

The Intermediate Shaft (IMS) bearing on 996 and 997-generation 911 models with the M96/M97 engine is a known weak point. Bearing failure causes catastrophic engine damage. We inspect and upgrade the IMS bearing on affected vehicles before failure occurs — far more cost-effective than a full engine rebuild.

?? Porsche Taycan Thermal Management

The Taycan's complex thermal management system — handling battery, motor and cabin cooling — can develop faults in Dubai's extreme ambient temperatures. Reduced range, charging interruptions and cooling warnings are common. We have the equipment and training to service Taycan EV systems safely and correctly.
