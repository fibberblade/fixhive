Ferrari Service Cost in Dubai — What You Actually Pay & Where Your Money Goes

June 2026 · By Rania Farouk
← Back to Blog

Dubai has one of the highest concentrations of Ferraris per capita in the world. The 458 Italia, 488 GTB, F8 Tributo, Roma, Portofino, California T, SF90 Stradale — you see them daily on Sheikh Zayed Road and in Dubai Investment Park. What you hear less about is what they cost to maintain properly. This guide breaks down Ferrari service costs in Dubai honestly — including what you pay at the official dealer versus an independent Ferrari specialist.

Ferrari Service Intervals — What the Manufacturer Requires

Ferrari's service schedule is based on annual intervals and mileage — whichever comes first. Most UAE owners reach the annual trigger before the mileage limit (20,000 km on most models). The schedule distinguishes between minor and major services, with a more significant engine belt or chain service at specific intervals depending on the model.

Annual Service (Minor)

The annual service covers:

  • Engine oil and filter (Ferrari specifies Shell Helix Ultra or equivalent 5W-40/0W-40 full synthetic)
  • Brake fluid replacement (Ferrari recommends annual brake fluid change — the high-performance braking systems generate heat that degrades fluid faster than standard vehicles)
  • Battery maintenance (Ferraris are particularly susceptible to battery drain from the extensive electronics when unused)
  • Coolant level check
  • Multi-point inspection and electronic health scan

Annual service cost at the official Ferrari dealer in Dubai: typically 4,500–8,000 AED depending on model year and any additional items identified. At a qualified independent Ferrari specialist: 2,500–4,500 AED for the same scope of work.

Major Service

Every 3–4 years (or at the specified mileage interval), the major service adds:

  • Spark plug replacement (V8 engines have 8 plugs; V12 engines have 12 — all are iridium plugs at premium prices)
  • Air filter replacement
  • Gearbox fluid (DCT or F1 dual-clutch)
  • Transfer case fluid (where applicable)
  • Detailed brake system inspection including caliper condition and disc thickness
  • Full chassis and suspension check

Major service cost at the dealer: 8,000–18,000 AED. At a specialist: 5,000–12,000 AED.

The Belt or Chain Service — The Biggest Scheduled Cost

This is where Ferrari service costs diverge dramatically from other exotics and where many owners receive a shock.

Belt-Driven Engines (California T, older 458, F430 variants)

Ferraris with dry-sump V8 engines using toothed drive belts require belt replacement at specific intervals. The belts access the camshaft timing and the engine must be substantially disassembled for the replacement. On the Ferrari 458 Italia, for example, the belt service typically comes at the 7-year or 30,000 km interval — whichever occurs first.

At the official Ferrari dealer: 25,000–45,000 AED for the belt service (it is a very labour-intensive job). At a qualified independent specialist who has done many Ferrari belt services: 15,000–28,000 AED for the same specification work.

Chain-Driven Engines (488, F8, Roma, SF90)

Newer Ferrari V8 engines (beginning with the 488 GTB in 2015) use timing chains rather than belts. Chains are theoretically maintenance-free if oil changes are kept current — but the service schedule still requires an inspection of the chain tensioners and valve clearance check at specific intervals. The cost for this inspection is lower than a belt service but should not be ignored, particularly in Dubai's heat where engine oil degradation can accelerate chain wear.

Model-Specific Service Costs in Dubai

Ferrari 458 Italia / Spider

  • Annual service: 5,500–7,500 AED (dealer) / 3,000–4,500 AED (specialist)
  • Belt service (every 7 years/30,000 km): 30,000–45,000 AED (dealer) / 18,000–28,000 AED (specialist)
  • Clutch replacement (the F1 single-clutch gearbox clutch wears in Dubai's traffic): 12,000–20,000 AED

Ferrari 488 GTB / Spider / Pista

  • Annual service: 5,500–8,000 AED (dealer) / 3,200–5,000 AED (specialist)
  • Major service every 3 years: 10,000–16,000 AED (dealer) / 7,000–11,000 AED (specialist)
  • DCT gearbox service (every 4 years): 3,500–5,500 AED

Ferrari F8 Tributo / Spider

  • Annual service: 6,000–9,000 AED (dealer) / 3,500–5,500 AED (specialist)
  • Major service: 11,000–18,000 AED (dealer) / 7,500–13,000 AED (specialist)

Ferrari Roma / Portofino M

  • Annual service: 5,500–8,500 AED (dealer) / 3,500–5,000 AED (specialist)
  • Major service: 10,000–16,000 AED (dealer) / 7,000–11,000 AED (specialist)

What Drives Ferrari Service Costs High

  • Labour rates — Ferrari work requires experienced technicians with Ferrari-specific training or very significant hands-on experience. Labour rates reflect this.
  • Genuine parts — Ferrari filters, spark plugs and fluids are significantly more expensive than generic equivalents. For critical items like brake fluid and engine oil we always use the specified products.
  • Special tooling — many Ferrari jobs require factory-specific tooling. An independent specialist who has invested in Ferrari tooling is not more expensive than one who improvises.
  • Engine access — Ferrari mid-engine layouts require significant disassembly to access components that take 20 minutes to change on a standard car. The 458 belt service involves removing the rear bodywork, engine cover and significant ancillaries before the belts are even visible.

Dubai-Specific Considerations

Battery maintenance is critical for Ferraris in Dubai. Owners who use their Ferrari irregularly — which is common in the UAE, where many owners have multiple vehicles — will find the battery draining from the always-on electronic systems. A battery tender (maintenance charger) connected when parked prevents the drain that eventually causes cell damage. Ferrari batteries are 1,500–3,500 AED to replace.

Brake system heat in Dubai's summer is a real concern even for weekend cars. The combination of high ambient temperatures and aggressive braking (particularly on sporadic drives after the car has been sitting) causes brake fluid moisture absorption to accelerate. Annual brake fluid replacement is not optional on a Ferrari driven in the UAE — it is a safety requirement.

Tyre degradation is faster than in Europe. Performance tyres on a Ferrari (Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2, Pirelli P Zero Corsa) degrade faster in Dubai's heat. Owners who drive enthusiastically should expect to replace rear tyres every 15,000–20,000 km — sooner on powerful V8 cars.

Dealer vs Independent Specialist

The official Ferrari dealer in Dubai provides manufacturer-backed servicing, warranty maintenance and access to the Ferrari diagnostic system (DEIS/SD2). For cars under warranty, dealer servicing is required. For out-of-warranty Ferraris, a qualified independent specialist can perform the same work — using the same parts and equivalent diagnostic tools — at 25–40% lower cost.

The key criteria for an independent Ferrari specialist: demonstrated experience with the specific model, investment in Ferrari diagnostic capability, and a willingness to show you the work and parts used. We are transparent about all of this at FixHive.
